# Route Gen AI

Plain-English ride requests turned into verified cycling routes and interval spots near a rider's start.

## Language

### Interval spots

**Stretch**:
A measured piece of road, ridden in a stated direction: its length, grade, climbing, stops, surface, road class, and how long one pass takes at the rider's power.
_Avoid_: window, segment, section

**Spot**:
A Stretch the finder recommends for intervals, together with how far the rider travels to reach it and the road it is on.
_Avoid_: result, candidate, interval spot

**Rep**:
One hard effort at the rider's target power for a set time, such as one of five 4-minute VO2 efforts.
_Avoid_: interval (for a single effort), set

**Lap**:
One pass along a Stretch; a Stretch shorter than a Rep needs several Laps, with a turnaround between them.
_Avoid_: pass (as a count), loop

**Spoke**:
One direction searched outward from the rider's start; every Stretch the finder considers lies along a Spoke.
_Avoid_: ray, arm, direction
